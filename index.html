Meet the Sphynx

Part cat, part hot-water bottle, all personality. Here's what makes the breed special:

🍑

Peach-fuzz coat

Sphynx cats look hairless, but most have a fine, suede-like fuzz that feels a bit like a warm peach.

🔥

Warm to the touch

With no fur coat to hide behind, they feel toasty in your hands and love a sunny spot, a heated bed, or your lap.

💞

Social butterflies

Affectionate, playful, and curious, Sphynx cats usually want to be right in the middle of whatever you're doing.

🛁

Spa-day regulars

Skin oils build up without fur to absorb them, so they need regular baths plus ear and nail cleaning.

🧣

Sweater weather

They chill easily and can sunburn, so they're best as indoor cats with cozy blankets (and maybe a sweater or two).
